# Department intake checklist
Collect **field definitions, sanitized examples, ownership and real workflows**, not raw sensitive exports into Git.

## CBC sponsor / Troy
Top 3 must-have workflows; PDF mockup priorities; final approvals; whether project should be prototype, data dashboard or operational CRM.

## Finance
Contribution/export column definitions, approved sample report, reporting period, committed vs received, installments, in-kind values, event ROI and totals to reconcile.

## Sponsorship/partnerships
Sponsors and contacts, relationship owners, pipeline stages, proposal/renewal process, how follow-ups are assigned, how an opportunity closes.

## Membership
Member types, fees, renewal and lapse rules, engagement metrics (if included).

## Programs & recipients
Program/cohort structures, award types, graduation/employment definitions and data restrictions (if included).

## Mentorship
Mentor/mentee pairing and check-in workflow, satisfaction and health criteria (if included).

## Leadership/board
Top metrics, dashboard filters, existing sample reports, permission differences.

## Technical liaison
HubSpot company/contact counts and properties; relevant WordPress form destinations; Google Workspace access model; approved Drive document locations.

## Return from each owner
1. One realistic end-to-end workflow
2. Required fields and permitted values
3. A synthetic/redacted sample
4. Current report example (when relevant)
5. Calculation/approval owner
6. Open questions and data sensitivity
