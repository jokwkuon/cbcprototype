# Project charter — DRAFT
**Organization:** Calgary Black Chambers (CBC)  
**Partner:** Prospective Bow Valley College student group  
**Instructor / student count / capacity:** TBD  
**Target:** Academic term ending around April (exact year/dates to confirm)

## Problem
CBC has records across legacy HubSpot, WordPress, Google Workspace/Drive and departmental spreadsheets. Troy's *CBC Database Architecture 2026* proposes an integrated relationship and impact system.

## Outcomes
1. Understand and structure organizations, contacts and sponsorship records.
2. Produce a testable core operational workflow or a data/BI foundation, as approved.
3. Validate reports against CBC-defined formulas.
4. Hand over code, tests, documentation and ownership.

## Constraints
- Small amount of data, likely readily exportable to CSV/JSON.
- Student skills and hours unconfirmed.
- Legacy contacts may be stale; do not silently trust them.
- Production access to personal/financial records requires formal authorization.

## Acceptance
See [acceptance](../delivery/acceptance.md); no binding student delivery commitment exists until sponsor and instructor agree on scope.
